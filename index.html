Methodology

Transparent by default.

What the model does

Annual compounding with monthly contributions approximated at mid-year, adjustable contribution growth, fees, inflation and an optional one-time drawdown. Probability bands use each profile’s assumed return and volatility—not a promise or a full Monte Carlo simulation.

What disclosures do not show

Congressional disclosures report broad value ranges and may include spouse/dependent holdings. Form 13F generally covers certain U.S.-listed securities, not an investor’s entire economic portfolio. Profiles here are simplified educational proxies.
